## Picking one

Need it to survive a URL? [base64url](/encodings/base64url). An email body? [base64](/encodings/base64), which MIME already speaks. A person reading it aloud over a bad phone line? [Crockford's Base32](/encodings/base32-crockford) or [z-base-32](/encodings/z-base-32).

Something money gets sent to? Anything with a checksum. [Base58Check](/encodings/base58check) catches most typos, [bech32](/encodings/bech32) and [bech32m](/encodings/bech32m) catch any four. Plain [base58](/encodings/base58) catches none, and that's why it never travels alone in Bitcoin.

Matching what somebody else wrote? Their spec wins, every time. A QR code wants [base45](/encodings/base45). A PDF stream wants [Ascii85](/encodings/ascii85). ZeroMQ wants [Z85](/encodings/z85). They look alike and they're not interchangeable.

Squeezing bytes into the fewest printable characters? [basE91](/encodings/base91), at about 23% overhead. Nobody else will read it, so make sure you're the only reader.
